Submission Rules

Requirements

  • Video must show full completion from start to finish
  • Raw footage must be available upon request
  • No cheats, hacks, or glitches allowed
  • Level must be on the ranked list

Verification Process

  1. Submit your run with video link
  2. Wait for moderator review
  3. Provide raw footage if requested
  4. Run gets approved or rejected with notes
