What is FEDL?

FEDL stands for Fair Extreme Demon List. We strive to be the most up-to-date, fair, and least-restrictive demon list for Geometry Dash. Our goal is to provide accurate rankings while keeping the submission process accessible and transparent for all players.

Our Mission

Unlike other demon lists with complex rules and strict requirements, FEDL focuses on fairness and inclusivity. We aim to verify demons quickly, accept a wide variety of completion methods, and maintain an up-to-date ranking that reflects the current state of the game.

Features

  • Browse the List - Search and filter through all ranked demons by title or rank position.
  • Watch Runs - View completion videos directly on the list page for each demon.
  • Submit Runs - Send your own completions for review by the moderation team.
  • Roulette - Randomly select demons from the list to play or challenge yourself with.
  • Player Profiles - Track your submissions and see your history on the site.
  • Messages - Communicate with other players and receive updates on your submissions.

How It Works

The site pulls its ranking data from the official demon list and updates regularly as new demons are verified. When you submit a run, it goes through a review process where moderators check that your video meets the requirements outlined in the rules page.

Once approved, your run gets linked to the corresponding demon on the list, making it visible to everyone who visits the site.

Theme Options

FEDL includes 14 different themes to customize your viewing experience. Visit the Account page to choose from themes like Default, Light, Ocean, Cyberpunk, Matrix, Fire, Galaxy, and more!
