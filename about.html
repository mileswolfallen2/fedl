What is FEDL?

FEDL stands for Fair Extreme Demon List. We are a community-driven Geometry Dash demon list focused on providing the most up-to-date, fair, and least-restrictive rankings in the GD demon community.

Our goal is to make demon verification accessible to everyone while maintaining accurate and current rankings that reflect the ever-evolving state of the game.

How It Works

Players submit their demon completions through our run submission system. Each submission is reviewed by our moderation team to verify the run meets our guidelines. Once approved, the run is linked to the corresponding demon on our list.

The list is updated in real-time as new demons are verified, ensuring players always have access to the most current rankings.

Features

  • Browse the List - Search and filter through all ranked demons by title, level ID, or rank position. Jump between rank ranges for quick navigation.
  • Player Rankings - Track the top players based on their verified runs and total points earned.
  • Submit Runs - Submit your own completions with video links and notes for review by our moderators.
  • Roulette - Feeling adventurous? Spin the roulette to get a random demon from the list as your next challenge.
  • Guess Game - Test your demon knowledge by guessing if a level is ranked higher or lower than shown.
  • Messages - Receive updates on your submissions and communicate with the team.
  • Themes - Customize your experience with 14 different color themes including Light, Dark, Ocean, Cyberpunk, Fire, Galaxy, and more.

Moderation

Our moderation team reviews all submissions to ensure fairness and accuracy. We verify completion videos, check for compliance with our rules, and provide feedback when runs are rejected.

Moderators include wolf_reaper90, dioxyx, and steve. Contact us through the Contact page for any moderation-related questions.
