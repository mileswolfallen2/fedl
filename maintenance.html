Scheduled server maintenance

We're moving FEDL to a new home

FEDL is being migrated off its current home server onto a new, more powerful PC that will host it from now on. The site may be briefly unavailable for about an hour at some point between September 3 and September 9.

Good news

No data is lost

Accounts, lists, and submitted runs all carry over untouched. You don't need to do anything or re-register.

Impact

Short downtime

The site drops offline for roughly an hour once during the window, then comes back up on the new machine.

Why

A more powerful server

The upgrade gives FEDL more headroom, smoother performance, and a more reliable home going forward.

Window

Sep 3 – 9

We'll keep the actual offline stretch as short as possible — just long enough to bring everything up on the new PC.
