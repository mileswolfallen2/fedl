Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains how FEDL (Fair Extreme Demon List) collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our website.

What personal information do we collect?

When you create an account or log in using Google OAuth, we collect:

  • Your Google account ID
  • Your email address
  • Your name (as provided by Google)

When you create a local account, we collect:

  • Username
  • Password (stored as a hashed and salted value)

We also collect information about your submitted runs, including player name, level title, video URLs, completion percentage, and notes.

When do we collect information?

We collect information when you:

  • Create an account or log in
  • Submit a run for review
  • Send messages or bug reports

How do we use your information?

We use the information we collect to:

  • Authenticate your account and maintain sessions
  • Associate submitted runs with your account
  • Display your username on submitted runs and messages
  • Communicate with you about your submissions
  • Improve our website and services

How do we protect your information?

Your personal information is stored securely on our servers. Passwords are hashed and salted. We do not store plain-text passwords. Access to user data is limited to necessary operations.

We use HTTPS to encrypt data in transit.

Do we use 'cookies'?

Yes. We use session cookies to keep you logged in during your visit. These cookies are essential for the functionality of the site and expire when you close your browser.

Third-party disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties, except as necessary for the operation of our services (e.g., Google OAuth for authentication).

Submitted run data (player names, videos, etc.) may be publicly displayed on our website.

Third-party links

Our website may contain links to external sites (e.g., YouTube videos). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these sites.

Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our website. Google may use cookies and collect data about your visits to serve relevant ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ad Settings.

Data retention

We retain user account information as long as the account is active. Submitted runs are kept indefinitely unless requested for deletion.

Your rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information. Contact us to request changes.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy at any time. Changes will be posted on this page.

Contacting Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us through the contact form on our website.
