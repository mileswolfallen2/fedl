Support

Need Help?

Find answers to common questions below. If you still need assistance, use the contact page to reach out.

How do I submit a run?

Go to the Submit Run page, fill out the form with your video link, raw footage, and notes. Your submission will be reviewed by moderators.

Why was my run rejected?

Common reasons include missing raw footage, mod usage violations, or incomplete submission details. Check the Rules page for full requirements.

How are demons ranked?

Demon rankings are based on difficulty, creativity, and popularity. The list is regularly updated by our moderation team.

Can I suggest a level for the list?

Yes! Use the contact page to suggest levels. Include the level ID and why you think it deserves to be ranked.
